Group Assessment Guidelines

Designing valid and authentic group assessment — July 2026

An IOA is an unscripted, two‑way conversation between an assessor and student(s) — often linked to an authentic scenario or artefact — guided by semi‑structured questions.

🧭

Process & Product

Group or Team assessment provide many opportunities for students to learn through authentic experiences and develop their employability skills. However, group assessment can present challenges, particularly when students perceive uneven contributions across the group, such as ‘freeloaders’.

The real issue with the 'freeloader' problem is not group assessment per se but specifically the design of the task. Well-designed group assessment can help mitigate these issues by promoting clear expectations, shared accountability, and meaningful participation from all team members. Designing effective group assessments requires focusing on both the process (collaboration) and the product (final output) to mitigate freeloading.
